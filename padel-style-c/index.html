Academy Pathway

From seedling to bloom

Our academy grows with you, in four gentle stages from first hit to match play.

Stage 01

Seedling

Never held a racket. Welcome.

Beginner adult learning a first serve. We cover court geography, soft grip mechanics, gentle underhand feeds, and how the back glass becomes your ally.

filter_vintage Focus: Grip, Stance & Confidence
psychology Focus: Wall Rebounds & Rhythm
Stage 02

Sprout

Rallies, walls and basic rules.

Two players rallying slowly. Learn glass angles, basic volley footwork, serving rotation, and how to maintain continuous, enjoyable 8-shot rallies.

Stage 03

Leaf

Tactics, positioning and doubles play.

Doubles pair at the net. Synchronize with your partner, master the bandeja and chiquita overheads, and take control of the transition zone.

groups Focus: Doubles Chemistry & Bandeja
military_tech Focus: Match Strategy & The 'Por Tres'
Stage 04

Bloom

Competitive match play and fine-tuning.

Advanced player in a smash. Speed reading opponent patterns, defensive recovery from difficult wall rebounds, and match tournament stamina.
