Protocol 03 · Medical Diode Laser

Laser hair removal

State-of-the-art contact cooling technology tuned specifically to Mediterranean and Levant phototypes.

Private Treatment Suite

HEPA-filtered air circulation, warm linens, and silent cooling handpieces.

01

Treatment areas

from $45

Underarms, bikini, legs, arms, back, upper lip, chin and more. Facial zones take from 15 minutes with complete discretion.

02

The patch test

$20 (redeemable)

A small test spot 48 hours before your first session to check your skin's response. Redeemable against your first treatment course.

03

How many sessions

Most people need 6–8 sessions, spaced 4–8 weeks apart depending on the area, hormone profile, and hair density.

04

Between sessions

Hair sheds gently over 1–3 weeks. Avoid sun, waxing and plucking, and use our provided post-laser mineral calm fluid and SPF 50.
