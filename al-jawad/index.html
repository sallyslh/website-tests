How to order

Delivery to your door, or a quiet table with us. Both are simple.

Pick from the menu, one dish or many. Every item is packed in breathable, thermal kraft boxes that retain crust and heat.

02

Order online for delivery, or reserve a table by phone. We prepare every plate to the moment of departure.
